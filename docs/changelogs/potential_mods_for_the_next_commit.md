I've been thinking of doing a rework of the freeze status. I know how in Legends: Arceus, Freeze is replaced with Frostbite. I find of Freeze being similar to burn, but with a focus on SPC to be interesting. With that said, I'm thinking of making Freeze, here are the new Freeze mechanics:

- -25% to SPECIAL damaging moves and -25% to base speed, as opposed to -50% to PHYSICAL damaging moves for Burn and -50% speed for Paralysis. The speed drop is because a frozen Pokemon should have decreased mobility, but shouldn't have the chance to skip your turn, nor be as drastic as Paralysis. Same thing for the damage drop being less than Burn's, since there is a lot more going on with Freeze that hinders a player or enemy Pokemon
- Like previously, the Pokemon has a chance to be cured of the status naturally, lasting for a maximum of five turns
- Damage will be 1/8th of health, same as Poison and Burn
- Fire moves will thaw out a frozen Pokemon early
